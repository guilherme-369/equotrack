# EquoTrack

## Product definition

EquoTrack is an academic web MVP designed to demonstrate structured,
centralized and longitudinal recording of equine-assisted therapy sessions.

The prototype focuses on practitioners with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

Its purpose is to demonstrate how session information can be organized,
retrieved chronologically and consolidated to support professional reporting.

## Problem

Session information may be recorded or shared through informal and
inconsistent processes.

This can create:

- variation in record structure;
- variation in level of detail;
- fragmented information;
- difficulty retrieving historical observations;
- manual effort when consolidating information for reports.

EquoTrack demonstrates a possible information-system approach to this problem.

## Core workflow

Record session
→ Structure information
→ Store record
→ Retrieve longitudinal history
→ Consolidate records

## Prototype limitation

EquoTrack is an academic prototype.

The variables, categories and scales displayed in the interface are
demonstrative and must not be represented as clinically validated
assessment instruments.

The application must not generate diagnoses or unsupported clinical
interpretations.
